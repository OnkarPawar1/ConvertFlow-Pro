Good to know

Images export as JPEG, PNG and WebP. Imports depend on browser decoding. Animated images export one frame. SVG must be self-contained. Image exports omit original EXIF metadata.

Video exports: MP4 (H.264), WebM (VP9), GIF. Audio exports: MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A. Use Audio tools to extract sound from videos. Input codecs depend on FFmpeg support.

The video engine loads on demand (~32 MB). Processing can be slow and memory intensive. Media limit: 200 MB per file. Try short clips first and keep this tab open. Stop discards the current conversion. Files disappear on reload.

Quality controls JPEG/WebP and video compression. PNG and WAV are lossless. This is a local converter, not an AI chatbot.
